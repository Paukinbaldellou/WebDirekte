01The journey

From the Boqueria to the Eixample

2018

The adventure begins

April 3rd. We open at Carrer de les Cabres, 13 — fourteen square metres of seasonal cooking made in plain sight. Just Arnau and one cook to start.

2018

.direktevins is born

The .direktevins project takes off with Antonio Lopo.

2018

Chef of the Year

The first award. Also the Repsol Guide recommendation, a place in Macarfi's top 50, best openings of the year in El Periódico and Time Out's 10 best restaurants.

2019

Asian Culinary Awards

We win the award with our ramen of skate allipebre from la Ràpita. We enter the Michelin Guide as recommended.

2020

Pandemic: total adaptation

We close for a month and use it to take stock: weekly creativity sessions with the team, online cooking courses for the Bellart school and the Aula de la Boqueria, and we start foraging in the woods. In June we reopen, doubling team and capacity with our new terrace.

2021

10 World's Coolest Places

Direkte enters the Forbes list.

2022

En busca del sol

Movistar films the documentary in which we win the Repsol Sun.

2023

Looking ahead

We start looking for a new home.

2024

Carrer de París, 200

We find the space and the slow, endless works begin.

2025

From the Boqueria to the Eixample

On April 5th, with great sadness, we serve our last service at the Boqueria. On April 17th, with great excitement, we open DIREKTE in the Eixample. La Vanguardia's award for best restaurant initiative, the Slow Food Barcelona Snail and the T de Oro for best restaurant in Catalonia from Forbes' Tapas magazine follow.

Today

2026

We keep learning and improving, to be a little better than yesterday.

02El chef

Arnau Muñío

Our chef, Arnau Muñío, is backed by 25 years of craft. He started at the Joviat school in Manresa, studying cooking and service. He trained at Barcelona's late Dama — one Michelin star — and combined studies and work with chef Ramon Martínez, now José Andrés' right hand, from whom he says he learned to love the profession.

He then set off for Andalusia, to La Alquería at elBullihotel — two Michelin stars — where he ran the fish station for a year. Back in Catalonia he joined Comerç 24, where he was head chef for ten years and where they won the Michelin star.

He worked with Albert Adrià as an external chef, helping in the creativity workshop for several elBarri group restaurants. He then designed and launched La Barra de Carles Abellan, which opened the door to the Boqueria stall where DIREKTE was born.

03The family

The family that helps us grow.

04Our workshop

.direkteferments

From our fermentation and preserving workshop, tucked away upstairs, we bring out different products through the seasons, which we use to build the menus all year round.

We ferment, pickle and macerate in-house. Time and patience.

  • Misos from legumes, nuts, tomato…
  • Vinegars from seasonal fruit and vegetable scraps
  • Kombuchas from seasonal leaves
  • Naturally carbonated drinks
  • Water kefir
  • Kefir from different milks
  • Aged products
  • Tepaches from seasonal fruit scraps
  • Rye and rice ferments
  • Homemade liqueurs (plums, blackberries…)
  • Ratafia
  • Pacharán
  • Various lacto-ferments
  • Pickles of seasonal produce
  • Preserves
  • Jams
  • and much more
